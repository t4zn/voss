FAQ

Got questions?

Common questions before we kick off a build. Anything else? Email hello@taizunkaptan.com.

© Taizun Kaptan

What stack and tools do you use?

Most often TypeScript, React/Next.js, Python for orchestration, major model APIs, and vector stores when retrieval matters—chosen for your constraints, not trends.

How long does a typical milestone take?

Spikes and experiments: days. MVPs: a few weeks. Ongoing product work: monthly milestones with clear scope and demos.

Do you work remotely?

Yes—remote by default. I overlap with US and EU hours and keep async updates tight so distance never blocks momentum.

How does pricing work?

We start with a short discovery call, a written scope, and a shared tracker—goals, stakeholders, and success metrics upfront.

Do I need design or UX before we start?

Not required. I can pair with your design partner or bring in specialists when branding needs extra polish before launch.
